The questions I get asked

And the honest answers.

How long to receive my invitation?

Count 5 to 10 days depending on the formula, from the moment I have the date, the venues and the style. If it’s urgent, say so: we can often speed up.

What if I change my mind about the design?

Adjustments are unlimited before approval: colors, typography, texts, order of sections. We only publish when it’s right.

How long does the link stay online?

For life, no subscription. The link stays active after the event, for photos and memories.

Can we manage both sides and count the children?

Yes. The RSVP distinguishes the bride’s side and the groom’s side, each family, men and women, then adults and children. The summary is generated in one click for the caterer.

Does it work without installing an app?

Yes, it’s a simple link: it opens in the browser, on iPhone as on Android.
