What I don’t do

Better to say it right away.

  • Repair blindly. No “it’ll be the motherboard” without a test.
  • A surprise quote. The announced price is the price paid.
  • Sell hot air. If an antivirus is enough, I say so.
  • Make you dependent. I document what I do.

Frequently asked questions

Answers before you ask.

Is the quote really free?

Yes. You describe the need, I answer with a clear price before starting. The announced price is the price paid; if something unexpected changes things, I tell you before continuing, never after.

Where do you work?

On site in Jerusalem, and remotely for web, systems, network and consulting: in France, in Israel, anywhere there is a connection.

How fast do you reply?

I answer myself, within 24 working hours. WhatsApp is the fastest channel: a photo, two lines, and we move.

What if it can’t be repaired?

I tell you frankly after the diagnosis. When the official quote says “unrepairable”, there is sometimes still something to try — but I prefer not to bill hope.

Is my data protected during a repair?

Data backup is included when possible. And I document what I do, so you don’t depend on me.

Which languages do you speak?

French, Hebrew and English.
