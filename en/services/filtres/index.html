What is guaranteed, everywhere

The product floor, on every device — private browsing included.

No adult sites

On every device, private browsing included. It’s not a checkbox: it’s the floor, and it can’t be unchecked.

SafeSearch enforced

Search engines filter their results, with no option to remove it — even when opening a private window.

Images analyzed on the device

Images are examined locally. They don’t leave for us to look at them.

Nothing switches off silently

If protection stops, you are warned on the device, and the console sees it too. No invisible failure.

Why an allowlist

Blocking what’s forbidden isn’t enough: we allow what’s useful, and the rest doesn’t exist.

A watertight allowlist

Everything not authorized is blocked, including known workarounds: VPNs, encrypted DNS, proxy browsers, hard-coded addresses.

The filter runs at your place

On the device, not in a cloud. None of your requests goes through our servers: we only receive the unblock requests you choose to send.

Levels, not a switch

Seven cumulative levels, from the Essential base to messaging only. You change with a slider, and the floor stays the floor.

A team that answers

A useful site is blocked? One-click request, handled by the filtering team under a written charter — not a ticket that disappears.

On every device in the house

The same protection, from the child’s phone to the office computer. No reset needed to start.

What Aribaz Filter will not do

Better to say it right away.

  • Read your browsing. The filter runs on the device: no request comes back to us.
  • Sell you a VPN. It’s not one more tunnel, it’s a filter that decides before the page opens.
  • Promise the impossible. Without a supervision lock, an iPhone profile can still be removed: it’s written, not hidden.
  • Hide the price. The core is free forever; the subscription adds the levels and the team, and can be canceled from the console.
